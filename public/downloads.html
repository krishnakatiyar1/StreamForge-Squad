Govt. Scholarship Support

Samaj Kalyan UP Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme

VSICS Kanpur assists eligible SC, ST, OBC, General, and EWS students enrolled in BCA, MCA, BBA, and MBA programs in applying for government fee reimbursement and stipends under the UP State Government Scholarship Scheme.

Mandatory Documents Checklist for Scholarship Application:

  • ✓ Income Certificate (Aavya Patra)
  • ✓ Caste Certificate (Jati Patra)
  • ✓ Domicile Certificate (Nivas Patra)
  • ✓ High School & Intermediate Marksheets
  • ✓ Semester College Fee Receipt
  • ✓ Aadhar-Linked Bank Account Passbook
  • ✓ Enrollment / Roll Number Certificate
  • ✓ Passport Size Photographs
